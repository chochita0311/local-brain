## State And Interaction Contract

- X stays the observed timestamp projection. Generate Y once from stable Session identity and collision resolution in a fixed reference scale, independent of current camera, zoom or viewport. Identical scoped records reproduce their homes after reload. Data/scope changes may resolve collisions differently. Never seed from cwd, branch, title or inferred membership.
- Use small native source circles with at least 40px targets and short titles. Suppress overlapping captions at dense scales; keep every point, full title accessibility/preview and honest visible/whole counts. No aligned project rows or decorative star field.
- A click selects and previews; empty-space drag pans. A point drag past a movement threshold temporarily offsets the dragged point with bounded resistance. Nearby screen points respond gently according to geometric distance only. No relationship lines or persistent offsets.
- During displacement, mark the original anchor. Exact activity observations remain tied to their recorded times. Release runs a damped return to exact rest; idle has no continuing animation. Cancel, Escape, camera/scale change, resize or page hiding restores rest and releases capture. Drag completion cannot accidentally select a point.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: direct pointer feedback remains, neighbors stay still and release restores rest immediately. Keyboard chronological navigation, native controls and detail access work without dragging. Touch node dragging is bounded to node targets; empty-space vertical touch scrolling remains the document's.
- Persist only scope-specific camera, selection and page. New layout state may migrate the prior camera time/scale/selection but must discard old lane Y. Validate all saved values. Storage failure preserves usability.
- Existing inventory membership, activity GET, source/workspace/List-page state, selection preview, detail/back, empty/unknown/error/no-script fallbacks and all List/Workflow Focus/Auto Work behaviors keep their established contracts.
