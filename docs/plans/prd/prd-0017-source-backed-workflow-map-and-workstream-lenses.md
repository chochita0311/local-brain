### Proposed Work Unit And Autonomy Contract

`Reconstructed workstream` is a working product term for an inferred,
outcome-oriented effort, not an existing `workstreams` database row. Final
naming and storage ownership remain open until the revised boundary is accepted.

- One flow describes a desired change to a subject with a coherent outcome or
  completion condition. A topic, repository, directory, time window, ticket,
  Session, or pair of references is not automatically that unit.
- Investigation, implementation, verification, and smaller tickets contributing
  to one outcome are subordinate activities or branches. They need no manually
  created Thread. An independently resumable outcome may form a peer flow.
- A mixed Session may support several flows through different evidence spans.
  Membership does not copy the entire Session's intent or unrelated artifacts.
- The product generates a source-attributed or explicitly inferred working
  label and incorporates new supporting activity without routine confirmation.
  Unknown fields stay unknown; a name, outcome, or next action is not invented
  merely to avoid an empty field.
- Uncertain material remains accessible as unassigned evidence with an honest
  coverage count. It does not become a new flow or an approval request by
  default. Excessive abstention is a quality failure, not a way to pass the
  small-count requirement.
- No global fixed number of flows is promised. Distinct outcomes remain
  distinct. Semantic consolidation, the current-attention view, and historical
  retention are separate responsibilities; pagination or a top-N display does
  not reduce the actual number of inferred flows or hide unexamined scope.
- Recent activity, supported open work, explicit pause/closure, and unknown
  lifecycle remain separate. Lack of recent activity alone neither closes nor
  deletes an effort. Routine use must require zero create/accept/promote actions.

### Proposed Extraction Decisions

This is an accepted experiment direction, not an implemented algorithm or
blanket permission to analyze private content. The bounded experiment must turn
these decisions into a tested contract before a production producer is built.

1. Extract evidence-scoped work statements: subject, desired change, stated
   completion condition, current action, outcome, and explicit continuation or
   contribution references. Retain source location, time, and observed versus
   inferred authority for each field; missing fields do not acquire defaults.
2. Prefer explicit goal-document identity, actually stored parent/child or
   continuation relations, and compatible scoped outcomes for grouping.
   Merely sharing a document, project, branch, common wording, or time is
   supporting evidence, not proof of one goal. The current schema does not
   guarantee a canonical goal or Jira-parent relation for every record.
3. Place contributing activities under a compatible existing inferred flow
   when evidence supports that outcome. New top-level flows require evidence
   of an independent outcome; lack of a match alone does not justify creation.
   Conflicting subjects/completion conditions prevent a merge. Similarity alone
   cannot assert causality, nor may a mixed Session transitively join efforts.
4. Preserve flow continuity as supporting tickets and documents change.
   Reprocessing unchanged facts must be stable; changed evidence may revise an
   inference with inspectable lineage, never silently retarget a correction or
   reassign a user-owned legacy record. Model labels and artifact-pair hashes
   are not the final flow identity.

Whether bounded non-model extraction can recognize sufficiently varied goal
language remains unproven. Semantic grouping, if later admitted, must face the
same contradiction, provenance, stability, and abstention tests. No numeric
similarity threshold or model is selected by this PRD.

### Delivered: Source-Neutral Evidence Admission

- Every source class already available through LocalBrain's approved runtime
  contracts is eligible to contribute evidence when the active projection has

    checkpoints, and other explicit user-owned organization relevant to the
    selected subject;

    freshness already present in LocalBrain;
  - current repository, commit, pull-request, conversation, mail, runtime, or
    other source evidence only after its own approved adapter and persistence
    contract exists.
- `Accessible` means eligible persisted local state or the result of an
  already explicit approved operation. Opening, focusing, filtering, or

  inspection, connected discovery, remote read, or Refresh.

  Atlassian local evidence Sync, Connections, discovery, and Refresh retain
  their separate action, authority, progress, and failure contracts.

  the map into a radial inventory.
- Source identity, source role, evidence kind, freshness, availability, and

### Delivered: Initial Episode And Workflow Projection

- One eligible primary work Session is the initial Episode anchor. Direct
  Subsessions remain subordinate evidence of their eligible parent and do not
  become top-level workflow lines. Maintenance and provider-internal Sessions
  remain outside ordinary workflow projection.
- An Episode is a source-backed observation of intent, subject, action,
  evidence, outcome, and next-action signals. The initial contract may leave
  unsupported fields unknown rather than generating them.
- Direction is time-ordered. A candidate relation must carry one or more
  inspectable reasons such as an explicit reference, Session parentage,
  user-confirmed organization, shared stable external identity, source-observed
  Git identity, or a bounded transition signal.
- Same workspace, directory, repository, lexical wording, or recency may
  support a candidate but never establishes a directional relation by itself.
- The delivered directional vocabulary is `continues`, `branches-from`, and
  `merged-into`. Contribution and parallelism remain possible later relations;
  grouping two activities by outcome does not itself prove a directional edge.
- Workflow origin distinguishes `earliest observed` from a user-confirmed
  start. New evidence may extend the observed origin backward without rewriting
  a user-confirmed boundary silently.
- Lack of recent activity means `quiet` or `last observed`, never `done`.
  Closure requires explicit completion, merge, abandonment, supersession, or a
  user confirmation and retains the closing reason and evidence.
- Lifecycle, recent activity, and epistemic authority remain separate axes.
  Inferred, directly observed, and user-confirmed state must not collapse into
  one badge or color.

### Proposed: Automatic Flows, Lenses, And Branches

- The [work-unit contract](#proposed-work-unit-and-autonomy-contract) owns the
  replacement's granularity and automatic behavior. A lens means inspecting
  one already usable inferred flow, not a post-promotion product mode.
- Flow membership may span disconnected observed Episodes and source families.
  Missing lineage evidence stays missing; the product does not fabricate
  connections to make one effort fit a connected-component rule.
- `Main`, when supported, is relative to the selected flow's outcome: the path
  that most directly advances that outcome. A supporting branch contributes an
  independently useful result to that path. A semantically similar but
  causally independent effort remains parallel or outside the lens.
- Main/sub presentation is derived from directional relations and the selected
  lens. It is not a universal property stored on every Episode.
- The product places subordinate activity automatically. A brief detour does
  not automatically become a peer flow; evidence of a distinct outcome may
  justify one without asking the user to promote every branch.
- Existing organization follows the [transition boundary](#legacy-transition-boundary).
  No existing Thread is silently reinterpreted as an automatic branch.

### Shared Workflow Map Interaction

Session Focus and Trace are delivered. Automatic overview and flow detail are
proposed core replacement experiences after extraction validation; a global
Atlas renderer and topic terrain remain optional later presentation decisions.

- The target information model is a directed acyclic graph that may branch and
  merge. The presentation should feel tree-like during local expansion while
  preserving merges that a strict tree cannot represent.
- Time provides a stable primary direction. Layout must not rely on continuous
  force simulation or allow ordinary focus changes to randomize the user's
  spatial memory.
- The target visual grammar has three distinguishable layers:
  - `flow spine`: time-directed Episode lineage, branch, merge, and terminal
    structure;
  - `evidence constellation`: source artifacts revealed around the selected
    Episode without competing with the lineage by default;
  - `topic terrain`: optional, non-authoritative background grouping that never
    defines a branch or Workstream.
- The target navigation model has three semantic scales that may be delivered
  in separate child Features:
  - `Atlas`: an optional global view over already usable automatic flows;
  - `Focus`: one selected Episode or Workstream lineage plus bounded branches;
  - `Trace`: evidence, relation reasons, source state, and destination links for
    one Episode or edge.
- Selecting an Episode keeps its position stable, illuminates its relevant
  lineage, reveals a bounded neighborhood and evidence constellation, dims
  unrelated context without removing orientation, and exposes an adjacent
  explanation of why each visible relation exists.
- Candidate and confirmed relations remain distinguishable without relying on
  color alone. Selection glow or motion may reinforce state but cannot be the
  only carrier of meaning.
- Map controls include visible zoom, fit, and reset behavior plus keyboard and
  non-pointer access. Ordinary page scrolling must not be captured as canvas
  zoom without the approved modifier or pinch interaction.
- A readable list or trace fallback preserves navigation and evidence when the
  diagram is unavailable, reduced, or unsuitable at a narrow viewport.

### Delivered: Bounded User Correction

- The map may offer reviewable corrections such as `same flow`, `split here`,
  `merge into`, `close`, and `reopen` after their ownership and persistence
  contract is approved.
- A correction changes only the user-owned assertion layer. It never rewrites
  source Sessions, documents, external systems, or direct observation evidence.
- User assertions survive rebuilds and model changes, are reversible, retain
  provenance, and do not treat silence, snooze, dismissal, or an unopened
  candidate as a correctness label.
- The initial read-only Focus projection may precede correction persistence if
  the foundation boundary is split, but the PRD is not satisfied until the user
  can inspect and correct consequential branch boundaries without manually
  rebuilding the map.

### Delivered Baseline And Proposed Analysis Comparison

- The initial workflow projection, Focus map, source explanations, and user
  correction path require no Qwen model, embedding runtime, external model, or
  network enrichment.
- No model is downloaded, installed, started, or invoked as a side effect of
  source ingestion, application startup, map entry, focus, search, or ordinary
  navigation.
- The proposed first comparison adds only bounded local text analysis after
  its boundary is approved. If it misses goal-language or continuity cases,

- Optional model outputs remain versioned, rebuildable inferences, not

- A model requires explicit admission, local resource and latency bounds,

| Routine intervention | Zero required creation, assignment, naming, acceptance, or promotion actions on entry and ordinary updates; no mandatory candidate queue |
| Safety and negative controls | All mandatory synthetic scenarios pass; zero merges across explicitly incompatible outcomes, source-policy violations, or legacy writes |
| Goal coverage | At least 80% of independently identified expected efforts are recovered as recognizable outcome-level flows; report unresolved expectations and unexamined input separately |
| Incorrect membership | At most 10% of assessed evidence-to-flow assignments contradict the independently established outcome; count shared/multi-goal cases against their scoped evidence, not whole Sessions |
| Fragmentation and count | At most one excess top-level group per ten recovered expected efforts; report actual/expected group counts and worst fragmentation before display filtering; do not impose a universal fixed cluster count |
| Correction burden | At least 90% of recovered expected efforts need no rename/join/separate/reassignment correction to be recognizable and resumable; missing efforts remain coverage failures and never count as zero-effort successes |
| Stability | Identical inputs produce identical results; unrelated append leaves at least 95% of prior assignments and identities unchanged; explicit corrections retain exact scope in every test |

Record local duration, peak memory, examined/unexamined input, analysis version,
and source availability alongside quality. Exact hardware-aware resource caps
belong to the approved experiment boundary. Passing a small sample does not
prove whole-history coverage, production latency, or a safe legacy migration.

#### Exit And Failure Route

- Pass: propose a production identity/authority/update foundation, then the
  automatic overview/flow-detail replacement and controlled legacy transition.
  The owner reviews a concise evidence summary, not a queue of memberships.
- Poor grouping or high correction burden: revisit work-unit/evidence/extraction
  rules. Do not build a review UI to transfer the unresolved problem to the user.
- Missing semantic capability: report the measured gap and propose a separately
  bounded local-model comparison; do not invoke or install one automatically.
- Insufficient sources or exceeded processing limits: report the exact evidence
  gap. No global success claim, hidden source access, or arbitrary truncation.
