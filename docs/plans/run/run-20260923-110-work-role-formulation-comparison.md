## Primary Review

Every role-set result and all six control outputs/traces were reviewed.

- Direct selection consistently distinguishes the single goal, success report,
  explicit outstanding check and performed check, where property questions add
  unsupported roles. This is a narrow improvement, not a new production default.
- It also loses information: action-plus-result and action-plus-pending omit the
  performed action in both orders; a planned check becomes a goal or goal/action;
  a failed result becomes remaining work. A bare edit still gains a goal.
- Neither method handles the fulfilled-pending contrast correctly. When the same
  work is later completed, the earlier pending sentence stays in remaining work;
  later completed action/result roles can also leak into that earlier sentence.
  Completion of different work correctly leaves the task pending in some forms.
- Order stability is insufficient: direct selection has nine recognized stable
  cases, three of them wrong; properties have five stable cases, three wrong.
  No voting, intersection, preferred order or repaired output is promoted.
- Unchanged relationship controls pass 5/6 final labels. Topic-only reading and
  listening are still falsely linked as continuation; selected original quotes
  and the same-model audit do not make that link supported. Contextless resumption
  finally abstains because its left citation is missing, but its intermediate
  `distinct_goals` decision is unsupported. The grounded short resumption retains
  a pronoun-only focus as supposedly concrete goal evidence. Correct final labels
  do not prove all intermediate judgments are sound.

The direct formulation reduces over-assignment but introduces omissions; it
does not solve extraction, lifecycle reconciliation or false continuity. No
holdout, private corpus, work-area clustering or map acceptance is established.
