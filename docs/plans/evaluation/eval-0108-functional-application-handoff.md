## What Improved And What Stayed Bounded

The same-input revised answers converge on the same candidate and delivery mechanism. Their wording and checks differ: one checks the resulting example and discusses conditional continued use; the other explicitly checks the delivered instruction and target record and ends after one response. Both distinguish setup from execution and disclose repeated manual work across Sessions. Neither invents a file path, automatic loading, skill installation or a new execution gate.

The baseline already supplied a concrete comparison, respected single-observation limits and checked normal clarification and acceptance. Those strengths remain; they are not counted as newly gained improvements. The observed gain is a reviewable application route. Longer prose by itself earns no credit.

The primary initially flagged the first revised answer's future adoption route as less explicit. Independent review and reconciliation found no mandatory gap: it proposes only a bounded direct-message trial, conditions continued use on further evidence and owner choice, and marks placement/loading unknown. Requiring a permanent destination here would exceed the supported intervention. The second answer's direct instruction-delivery check is an available stronger check, not a reason to force another generation.

[Contract checks](eval-0108-contract-application-handoff.md) separately pass all first outputs, 176 integrity/schema/rendering assertions and 24 focused tests.

This deliberate sample does not replay production retrieval, evaluate project ranking, or show stable behavior across broad tasks or all eleven types. Model, reasoning effort and seed were not independently resolved. No actual product CLI call, live account inspection or adoption was performed.

Application specificity is not proof of fewer user corrections, improved comprehension or reduced total effort. RUN-133's unproven incremental benefit remains unproven. Stop after the declared five first outputs and independent review, with no extra generation to force a result.
