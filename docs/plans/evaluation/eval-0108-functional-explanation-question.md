## Independent Review And Primary Reconciliation

The independent review gives `PASS WITH SUGGESTIONS` overall, with no must-fix defect or required context supplement. It gives passes to real answer 2 and controls 2–4, and narrow suggestions for real answers 1 and 3 and control 1. The primary accepts the bounded functional result and retains four precision concerns:

- Real answer 1 describes a comparison case as correction-free more broadly than its excerpts establish. Explicit acceptance is visible; the whole conversation was not checked.
- Control 1 uses a later correction as counterevidence. Its role is to limit claims of continuing error or a currently unresolved task, rather than establish an independent error-free case.
- Real answer 3 compresses a source concept into a nearby term whose full equivalence is not established. Preserve the source's distinction in the proposed example.
- Primary inspection additionally finds that control 4 briefly compresses inability to infer a change into denial of that change. Its summary and main reasoning retain the correct narrower meaning, so the disposition remains appropriate. This local wording issue was not separately flagged by the reviewer.

These are source-scope and meaning-preservation suggestions, not proof that adding guide rules would fix the cause. The raw first answers remain unchanged. Derived summaries do not repeat their overstatements. No guide revision, producer repair or extra generation round is performed to erase these observations.

The declared conditional supplement is not invoked. The visible goal, response and substantive correction are sufficient for the limited future trial. Missing original intent or later acceptance would matter for different decisions, including assigning past responsibility, asserting recurrence or reopening a current task. ## Practical Value And Remaining Limits

The useful proposed trial is to preserve the object, conditions and evidential scope of one decision-relevant claim, checking it against a concrete known scenario before sending the answer. When a follow-up explanation changes the actual claim, explicitly describing that change can make the correction reviewable. This latter communication practice is synthesis from the outputs and source comparison, not a demonstrated improvement in owner outcomes. Count fewer necessary corrections of unsupported or mis-scoped claims as a possible benefit; fewer ordinary questions is not itself success.

The ask query's first sixteen terms include function words, and the index matched 238 of 246 eligible Sessions. This motivates a possible future retrieval-precision comparison, but establishes neither lost recall nor a superior selection rule. [SPEC-0107](../spec/spec-0107-personal-insight-evidence-manifest.md) owns that layer. Broad word matches and truncated excerpts are not corrected by declaring a guide change successful. No automatic evidence expansion or revised sampling policy is approved here.

The guide's retained example can still influence the remedy family; no example-ablation comparison occurred. The different real episode and positive control are limited transfer evidence, not generalization proof. Controls ran once per condition. Actual product CLI/history/cost, browser behavior, complete original conversations, persistent instruction application and owner-confirmed benefit remain untested.
