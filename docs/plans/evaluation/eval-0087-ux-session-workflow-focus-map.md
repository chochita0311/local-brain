## Checks And Evidence

- The separate Focus route answers three primary questions without requiring a
  manual Workstream: what led to this Session, what continued or branched, and
  why the product drew each connection. The initial focus remains the Session
  from which the user entered.
- Stable time direction and branch lanes provide orientation without force
  motion. Selecting an Episode illuminates only its supported path, keeps the
  card spatially fixed, and updates the adjacent Trace, so inspection does not
  destroy the user's mental map.
- Branch disclosure reveals only already-retained projected Episodes and cannot
  hide a selected descendant. An unconnected Session remains a useful one-node
  state and is not mislabeled as complete or erroneous.
- Relation labels, authority, and full reasons separate deterministic candidates
  from future user confirmation. Evidence is grouped by source family beneath
  the Episode, includes freshness/unavailable language and safe destinations,
  and never competes with Episodes as map structure.
- Browser history, direct canonical routes, and source-detail return preserve
  focus. Keyboard Episode and edge actions, visible zoom/reset/fit controls,
  reduced-motion semantics, and textual fallback avoid a pointer-only or
  graph-only dead end.
- Ordinary wheel movement remains available to the page or bounded viewport;
  only modifier-wheel/pinch owns zoom. Narrow layouts choose readable textual
  lineage and Trace over a compressed miniature graph, with no document-level
  horizontal overflow at `700` or `320`.
