# Decision memory · v1 (`decision_memory`)

- **Trigger:** Comparable alternatives are discussed again because an earlier rationale or revisit trigger is hard to recover.
- **Exclude:** A changed circumstance, intentionally tentative earlier choice, or legitimate periodic reassessment.
- **Evidence questions:** Are distinct dated discussions visible? Was a choice actually made? What new evidence could justify revisiting it?
- **Benign alternative:** Reopening a decision can be responsible when constraints change.
- **Intervention:** Keep a compact dated note with choice, alternatives, rationale, evidence, and a revisit condition.
- **Output sketch:** Show the apparent repeated decision and a note shape that leaves uncertainty and reopening conditions visible.
- **Follow-up:** At the next related decision, ask whether the note clarified what still applies and what changed.
- **Current-source limit:** Do not infer final approval or missing documentation from sampled discussion alone; request the decision source when necessary.
