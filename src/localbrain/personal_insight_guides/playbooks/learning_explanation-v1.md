# Learning and explanation · v1 (`learning_explanation`)

- **Trigger:** The person asks what a term means, why a result occurred, or how a method works across relevant work.
- **Exclude:** Healthy curiosity, an explicit teaching goal, or confusion caused by an inaccurate AI answer.
- **Evidence questions:** Are the concept and question directly visible? Is a previous explanation available and correct? Does the person want understanding rather than speed?
- **Benign alternative:** Repeated questions can be deliberate practice or deeper inquiry.
- **Intervention:** Try a worked example, underlying mechanism, near-miss comparison, or brief self-check; keep a learning note only when it helps later use.
- **Output sketch:** Name the concept and one explanation format to try, without treating learning as wasted effort.
- **Follow-up:** Ask the person to explain or apply the concept in a new example.
- **Current-source limit:** A sampled question alone does not show whether the prior answer was correct or whether learning succeeded.
