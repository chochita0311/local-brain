# Personal value and work flow · v1 (`personal_value`)

- **Trigger:** Work is repeatedly started while the person's stated priority or desired outcome is not revisited.
- **Exclude:** Exploration, external blocks, off-Session completion, or an unstated goal inferred from activity volume.
- **Evidence questions:** Which priority did the person state? Is it still current? What work may have happened outside captured Sessions?
- **Benign alternative:** Several starts may represent useful discovery or parallel obligations.
- **Intervention:** Ask the owner what mattered, then try one small next outcome, a review point, or an explicit stop decision.
- **Output sketch:** Offer a reflection question and bounded next step, not a motivation or performance diagnosis.
- **Follow-up:** Ask whether the chosen outcome was completed or intentionally changed and whether priorities became clearer.
- **Current-source limit:** Sessions show only part of the person's work; do not infer health, motivation, job performance, or personal values from transcripts.
