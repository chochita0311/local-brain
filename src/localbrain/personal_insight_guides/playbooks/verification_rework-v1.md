# Verification and rework · v1 (`verification_rework`)

- **Trigger:** A claim or artifact is corrected late after being treated as reliable.
- **Exclude:** A revision caused by changed requirements, creative iteration, or a correction that the source does not substantiate.
- **Evidence questions:** Is the initial claim and later correction visible? What authoritative source was available? What consequence is actually known?
- **Benign alternative:** Revision may be a productive review step rather than avoidable rework.
- **Intervention:** Move one source comparison, assumption check, or criterion earlier; for recurring objective errors, create a focused evaluation example.
- **Output sketch:** State the visible correction, uncertainty about cause, and one earlier check to test.
- **Follow-up:** On the next comparable task, record whether the check catches a material error before downstream work.
- **Current-source limit:** Normalized messages do not prove tool success, final artifact correctness, or prevented errors; request outcome evidence before making those claims.
